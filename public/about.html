About

What Coptic Synaxis is, and what you can reuse.

Coptic Synaxis is a liturgical companion for the Coptic Orthodox year: feast and season timelines, the day’s katamaros references, and bilingual scripture reading.

Built as packages first

The calendar and Bible lookup are not locked inside this UI. The same logic ships as coptic-readings and bible-citation-text so other apps, sites, and tools can depend on them.

What you get in the app

  • Today’s Coptic date, feast, and tune
  • Year view of seasons and feasts with past / now / coming
  • Day readings as citations, opened in a bilingual viewer
  • Free Bible lookup by book and verse range

For developers

Install the packages from npm, wire them into your stack, and keep liturgical dates and scripture resolution consistent across products. Full install snippets and examples live on the Packages page.
