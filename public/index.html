Open packages

The same libraries behind the app — npm, yarn, pnpm, or bun. ESM, CommonJS, and TypeScript types.

npm

coptic-readings

Given a date, get katamaros reading references for that liturgical day — vespers, matins, liturgy — plus feast, tune, and Coptic calendar context.

npm

bible-citation-text

Turn citation strings like John 20:1-18 into English and Arabic verse text from public chapter assets.

Better together

Use coptic-readings for the day’s refs, then bible-citation-text to resolve Psalms, Gospels, and the rest into readable text.
